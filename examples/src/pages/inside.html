Inside a Split

A gutter binds the arrows, Home, End, the Page keys and Enter — and so does a <select>, a scroll container, and half the component library.

Nothing collides, because the gutter's listener is on the gutter and pane content is its sibling, not its descendant. Events from the content never travel through it.
